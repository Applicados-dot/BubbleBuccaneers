Bubble Buccaneers Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 · App version: 1.0.0

Bubble Buccaneers (package name: com.bubblebuccaneers.arcade) is a free arcade game supported by advertisements. This Privacy Policy explains what data may be collected and how it is used.

1. Data Controller

Developer: Applicados
Contact: infogooweb@gmail.com

2. About the Application

Bubble Buccaneers is a classic arcade action game inspired by the legendary bubble-shooting and harpoon games. Players take control of a pirate and must destroy bouncing bubbles that split into smaller ones, progressing through campaign levels, time attack challenges, and endless survival mode.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not request or store names, email addresses, or account information.
  • No user registration is required.
  • Scores, unlocks, coins, settings, and game progress are stored only on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that store personal user data.

4. Data That May Be Collected by Third Parties (Advertising)

The application displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertising services, including:

  • Advertising device identifier.
  • Basic device information (model, language, and operating system).
  • Application performance and diagnostic data.
  • Interactions with displayed advertisements.
  • Approximate IP address for security and legal compliance purposes.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

Where required by applicable law, users will be asked for consent before personalized advertisements are shown.

5. App Permissions

  • Internet Access: required to load advertisements.
  • Vibration: used for gameplay feedback when enabled.

6. Children

Bubble Buccaneers is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

7. Data Retention and Security

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data processed by advertising providers is managed according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, or objection to the processing of your personal data. For matters related to advertising data, please contact Google directly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact: infogooweb@gmail.com
